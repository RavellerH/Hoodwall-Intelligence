## Thesis

A single thematic launch wave on the Robinhood Chain / pons launchpad, not
several independent narratives. Tokens are cloning whichever theme is
currently getting volume — overwhelmingly "Robinhood", tokenized stocks,
prediction markets and launchpad infrastructure — which is the signature of
a launchpad-driven meme cycle rather than fundamentals-driven interest.

## Evidence

Narrative distribution across 58 token cards in the 2026-09-08 window:

| Bucket | Tokens | Character |
|---|---|---|
| platform | 12 | Parody "infra": prediction markets, DeFi terminals, launchpads |
| brand-riff | 4 | Piggybacking a real brand (Robinhood, GameStop, CNBC-style) |
| culture-meme | 4 | Internet-culture bit, no brand tie |
| defi | 3 | — |
| person-meme | 1 | — |
| animal-meme | 1 | — |

`platform` and `brand-riff` together are more than half of all tagged
tokens, and nearly all reuse the same handful of buzzwords.

Median market cap at signal was $86,200 (range $23,700–$1,830,000): these
are micro caps where a single exit moves the price.

## Why conviction is low

The vendor's own 24h scoreboard reports a median outcome of **0.23x** — the
typical called token is down about 77% — with only 6.6% still at 2x. The
`brand-riff` cluster additionally carries legal and reputational risk: those
tokens trade on the name recognition of unrelated real companies, with no
affiliation implied or likely genuine.

## What would change this view

- Median multiple recovering above 1.0x across a full window.
- Narrative diversity rising (the `platform` share falling below ~25%).
- Tokens sustaining market cap beyond the first 24 hours rather than
  round-tripping.
